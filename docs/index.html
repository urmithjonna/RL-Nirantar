Nirantar — keeping the lights on with reinforcement learning

A PPO controller for a solar + battery system shared by 10 homes on a street with frequent, home-by-home power cuts. Trained on real outage and load data from three Indian regions, warm-started by imitating a perfect-foresight optimiser (behaviour cloning + DAgger), protected by a 4-hour safety shield, and tested once on an unseen city — Pune 2018.

How it works

1 · Data164 homes, ~9.2 M 15-minute home-steps: CEEW smart meters (Mathura, Bareilly), Prayas eMARC + ESMI (Uttar Pradesh, Pune), NASA POWER solar.
2 · ForecastsPer-home cut risk and demand at +1/+2/+4 h (CatBoost, XGBoost, LightGBM), admitted only if ≥ 5% better than baselines.
3 · ImitationA perfect-foresight MILP expert labels 658,560 states; behaviour cloning + 2 DAgger rounds give the starting policy.
4 · PPO + shield1 M PPO steps per run; MLP vs GRU (last 8 states), 5 seeds each; the shield keeps ≥ 4 h of essential reserve.
5 · One testEverything chosen on 73 validation weeks; Pune 2018 (50 weeks) run once with paired bootstrap CIs.
